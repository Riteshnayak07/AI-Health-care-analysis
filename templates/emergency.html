Step-by-Step Life Support & First Aid

Standard protocols while emergency medical personnel are en route

🫀 Adult CPR (Hands-Only)

  1. Check for responsiveness and breathing.
  2. Place hands interlocked in center of the chest.
  3. Push hard and fast: 100-120 beats per minute (to the tempo of 'Stayin' Alive').
  4. Allow chest to recoil fully between compressions until help arrives.

🗣️ Choking (Heimlich Maneuver)

  1. Stand behind person and wrap arms around their waist.
  2. Make a fist with one hand just above the navel.
  3. Grasp fist with other hand and give quick upward inward thrusts.
  4. Repeat until airway obstruction clears.

🩸 Severe Bleeding & Hemorrhage

  1. Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth or sterile gauze.
  2. Do not remove soaked cloth; add more layers on top.
  3. Keep the injured limb elevated above the heart level if possible.
  4. Maintain uninterrupted pressure until paramedics arrive.

🔥 Burns & Scalds

  1. Immediately cool under gentle running tap water for 10-20 minutes.
  2. Never apply ice, butter, or oil directly onto burned skin.
  3. Cover loosely with sterile non-stick plastic wrap or gauze.
  4. Do not pop blisters or remove clothing stuck to burned areas.
